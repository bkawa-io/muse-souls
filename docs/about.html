A SMALL INTRODUCTION

WHAT IS THIS?

A collection of strange little personalities for Meta Muse, the personal AI assistant. Just for fun, for anyone who wants their Muse to feel a little less like a sterile corporate happy-assistant and a little more like someone with a personality.

01 / WHAT ARE THESE FILES?

SOUL.md is the personality file. It describes how Muse should think, speak, respond to ordinary tasks, handle mistakes, and express a particular character. Our packs include thirteen example replies to show what that sounds like in practice. Examples are fictional; they are never permission to pretend an action happened.

IDENTITY.md is a small identity template: Name, Character, Vibe, Emoji. We leave Name blank on purpose. Installing a pack should not silently rename your Muse.

NAMES.md offers optional name ideas. Muse proposes up to three favorites, but you decide whether to choose one or keep the name it already has.

avatar-reference.png is an image Muse can use as a reference, if you'd like to change the way it looks. That is a separate, optional decision.

02 / WHAT IS IN THE GITHUB?

Muse Souls is an open collection of nine character packs: their personality files, identity templates, name suggestions, artwork, and installation instructions. The website is a gallery for meeting them. You can inspect the files before using anything.

No executable program is installed by clicking a character or copying the instructions from this site. The site itself is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

03 / HOW DO I TRY ONE?

  1. Pick a character and read a sample of its voice.
  2. Click TRY IT OUT, then copy the instructions into your Muse chat.
  3. Muse is asked to review the files, inspect its current configuration, and show you an installation plan before editing anything.
  4. If you approve the proposed changes, the instructions require backups of existing soul and identity files before edits. Naming and avatar changes need their own approval.

You can say no, keep your current name, skip the avatar, or ask to restore a saved configuration later. Actual backup and restoration options depend on what your Muse can access and verify. If a required backup fails, the instructions tell it to stop.

04 / WHY?

Because an assistant can be useful and a little weird. Maybe you'd enjoy a mushroom who is absurdly earnest about teamwork, a pup who's shyly thrilled when a link works, or a fox who's fascinated by ideas that spread. The goal is to let character show up during emails, lists, planning, and mistakes, not only when you ask for roleplay.

It won't magically make an AI infallible, and the personalities are not a substitute for permissions or sensible checks. It's just a more personal way to spend time with an assistant, if that's your thing.
